IUN-PKF INTEL DOSSIER // WINGS OF THEVE

Who are the Wings of Theve (Wings of Thieves)?

The Wings of Theve is an elite coalition mercenary squadron formed in the aftermath of the Lighthouse War. Disavowed by national governments, they operate cutting-edge experimental 6th-gen fighters to neutralize rogue AI networks and secure autonomous weapons before they fall into Belkan syndicate hands.

Captain Tasha Vance
Codename: "Valkyrie 1" // Squadron Commander
Ace Pilot

A veteran carrier aviator formerly assigned to OADF 108th Tactical Fighter Wing. Known for her aggressive close-range gun kills and decisive tactical leadership in heavy electronic warfare environments.

SIGNATURE FIGHTER: FA-36 Sovereign / F/A-18F
1st Lt. Kate Miller
Codename: "Siren" // Tactical Electronic Officer & Wingman
EW Specialist

The analytical core of the flight. Specializes in jamming enemy satellite missile uplinks and hacking automated drone defense grids in real-time during dogfights.

SIGNATURE FIGHTER: EA-18G Growler / Eurofighter
Keiki Kobayashi
Lead Composer // Bandai Namco Sound Team
Original Soundtrack

The legendary composer behind *Zero*, *Megalith*, and *Daredevil* returns to score Ace Combat 8, fusing symphonic orchestral choir with aggressive synth-rock and dynamic flight-responsive tempo changes.

KEY TRACKS: "Wings of Theve", "Dark Blue Zenith"
