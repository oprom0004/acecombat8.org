Frequently Asked Questions

Community questions regarding Ace Combat 8 aircraft mechanics and tech tree progressions.

How do I unlock the ADF-11F Raven in the Tech Tree?

The ADF-11F Raven requires purchasing the complete Belkan/Grunder tech branch (ADFX-01 and ADF-01 Falken) and accumulating 3,500,000 MRP after clearing Campaign Mission 20 on Hard difficulty or higher.

What is the best Special Weapon for multiplayer dogfights?

QAAM (Quick Air-to-Air Missile) remains the top choice for close-in high-G turn battles, while EML (Electromagnetic Railgun) provides instant 1-shot kills for pilots with pinpoint manual aim.

Does stealth rating prevent missile tracking?

Stealth reduces enemy radar lock-on distance from 4,000m to 1,200m and breaks locks faster during cloud penetration, but does not alter missile homing once a missile is already tracking.

Can I practice dogfights without the full game?

Yes! Check out our sister site playacecombat8.com/play-online/ to play a zero-install 3D tactical dogfight game right inside your desktop or mobile browser.
