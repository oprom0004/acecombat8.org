Flagship Aircraft Profiles

Tactical breakdown and combat role recommendations.

F-22A Raptor
The Apex Air Superiority Platform
Tier S

Combines unmatched stealth radar cross-section with thrust-vectoring supercruise. Equipping QAAM turns the Raptor into the ultimate dogfight brawler for Multiplayer and high-difficulty Ace runs.

BEST UPGRADE PARTS: Light Blisk Lv.2 + Variable Flap
Su-57 Felon
Pugachev's Cobra Specialist
Tier S

With 3D thrust vectoring nozzles, the Su-57 offers superior instantaneous turn rates. Its Pulse Laser (PLSL) melts boss shields and heavy warships at 5,000m range with zero projectile drop.

BEST UPGRADE PARTS: Pulse Laser Focus Lens Lv.2
X-02S Strike Wyvern
Variable Geometry Superplane
Tier SS

Equipped with an internal spinal Electromagnetic Launcher (EML) capable of vaporizing enemy aces across 10,000m in a single frame. Outer wings fold dynamically during high-G turns.

BEST UPGRADE PARTS: Railgun Sniper Amplifier Lv.2
