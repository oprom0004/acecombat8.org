[TAC-DIRECTIVE] S-Rank Scoring Formula

In Ace Combat 8, your overall rank is computed via Total Points = Destruction Score + Time Bonus. Destroying high-value secondary targets (SAM sites, Aegis cruisers, and radar antennas) before the mission update is crucial to meet S-Rank thresholds.
