DIRECT ANSWER // MISSION STATS

How many missions are in Ace Combat 8 and how long does it take?

Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve features 20 main story missions. Completing the main storyline on Normal difficulty takes 12 to 15 hours. Achieving S-Ranks across all missions on Hard and Ace difficulty requires 30 to 45+ hours of total playtime.

STORY ONLY

Main Campaign (Casual / Normal)

12 - 15 Hours

Focuses purely on narrative progression, briefing cutscenes, and clearing missions with starter 4th-gen aircraft.

MAIN + EXTRAS

Hard Run + Tech Tree Unlocks

20 - 25 Hours

Unlocking 5th-gen fighters (F-22A, Su-57), downing named enemy aces, and earning MRP for aircraft parts.

COMPLETIONIST

Ace Difficulty S-Rank & 100% Medals

40 - 50 Hours

Unlocking the FA-36 Sovereign, ADF-11F Raven, acquiring all 42 aircraft skins, and mastering no-damage speedruns.
