What is the best plane in Ace Combat 8?

The overall best aircraft in Ace Combat 8 is the FA-36 Sovereign (6th Gen Superplane) for late-game supremacy, and the F-22A Raptor for the main campaign. For close-range multiplayer dogfights, the Su-57 Felon dominates due to 3D thrust vectoring, while the X-02S Strike Wyvern is the premier sniper with its instant-kill EML Railgun.
